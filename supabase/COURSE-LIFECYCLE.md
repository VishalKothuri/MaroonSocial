# Course chat calendar and retention

Course chat authorization is checked against `course_private.terms` on every read, write and join. The native bundle provides labels and an offline explanation; the database is authoritative. Spring opens December 1 of the previous year, Summer opens May 1, and Fall opens August 1, in `America/Chicago`. Unpublished or unverified ending dates keep a term locked.

The official source is the [general university academic calendar](https://catalog.tamu.edu/undergraduate/academic-calendar/). `functions/_shared/course-calendar.ts` reads only its general Texas A&M University / Galveston section. Dentistry, Qatar, professional calendars and Washington, D.C.-only exam rows are excluded. The last published final examination determines `endsOn`; the parser requires all three summer sessions and selects their latest final examination. It never substitutes final-grade deadlines or commencement dates. Ambiguous or missing terms are omitted from ingestion, preserving existing stored dates.

`course_calendar_ingest(p_terms)` derives the closing boundary as midnight after the last examination, then adds one calendar month in Chicago time for the purge boundary. Already closed terms cannot be reopened by a changed feed. The parser sends no client-derived opening or purge timestamps.

## Scheduled maintenance

The existing `maroon-campus-hourly` job runs at minute 17 of each hour. `refresh-campus` uses the existing 55-minute campus lease and calls `maintainCourseChats(db)` in its background work. Course-calendar refresh has its own database claim limiting attempts to once per day. A failed fetch preserves the previous calendar; it does not authorize guessed dates.

Logical access expires at the database closing boundary even before maintenance runs. Maintenance marks expired rooms closed, then purges rooms whose retention deadline passed. Each worker invocation processes at most five batches of 500 rooms and five batches of 100 queued Storage objects.

Physical deletion uses the authenticated Storage API, followed by `social_media_cleanup_complete`. Only an HTTP 200 Storage response permits acknowledgement. Network failures, non-200 responses and failed acknowledgements retain jobs for retry. Paths must match the UUID `.jpg`, `.png` or `.gif` filenames generated by the social-media upload endpoint. Invalid paths stop the batch without an outbound Storage request; operators should investigate them rather than clearing the queue.

The queue is shared with ordinary account-media cleanup. Course purges queue only attachments where `external_source IS NULL`. KLIPY reference metadata is removed with the room, but provider URLs/dummy paths never enter our Storage deletion queue. No database rows are removed directly from `storage.objects`.
