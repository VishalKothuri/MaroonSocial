Community guidelines

Maroon Social is a place for Aggies to talk honestly, ask for help and have fun, often anonymously. These guidelines apply to every post, reply, message request, chat and group in the app. You accept them before your first post, reply or message, and again when we publish a new version.

Be an Aggie

Treat people the way you’d want to be treated in the MSC: honest, respectful and willing to help. Disagree with ideas, not with people.

Leave other students out of it

Don’t name, initial or picture other students to mock or expose them. That includes nicknames, descriptions or photos that make someone easy to identify.

No harassment, hate or threats

No bullying, slurs, attacks on someone’s race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or national origin, threats of violence, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves.

Keep sexual content in its place

Nothing sexual outside the 18+ NSFW community. Inside it, only content involving consenting adults, shared with their consent. Never anything involving minors: we remove it, close the account and report it as the law requires.

No doxxing, spam, scams or illegal sales

Don’t share anyone’s private information (addresses, phone numbers, schedules, accounts, documents). Don’t flood feeds or chats, run scams, impersonate others, or sell or promote anything illegal, including drugs, weapons, fake IDs and academic dishonesty services.

Anonymity is not immunity

Other members can’t see who you are when you post anonymously, but your account is still accountable. We act on reports: we remove content, limit or suspend accounts, and respond to valid law-enforcement requests.

Report and block

Use Report on any post, reply, message or conversation to send it to moderators, and Block to stop someone from reaching you. Moderators review every report.

If you’re struggling

Call or text 988 (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7). In an emergency, call 911. If a post worries you about someone’s safety, report it so we can respond.

Contact

Questions, appeals or safety concerns: support@maroonsocial.chat. See also Privacy & safety.
