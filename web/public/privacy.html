Privacy & safety

Your identity

People in the lobby see your chosen lobby username and up to six interests while you are actively waiting. Your account backs blocks, reports, and enforcement. The service operator can investigate reports; this is not operator-unlinkable anonymity.

Direct video

After a connection request is accepted and both people confirm they are still present, video and audio travel directly between participants with your consent. The other participant can learn your network IP address. No paid relay is enabled. Some networks cannot connect directly; try a different Wi-Fi or mobile network, or use text.

Your conversation

Text is processed by the service and recent chat can be attached to a report. This service does not record video or audio. Another participant could record their own screen, so avoid sharing information you would not want saved.

Stay in control

End a conversation, block a participant, or report abuse using the controls beside the chat. Continuing in Inbox requires a separate request and the recipient’s acceptance. Unlink your browser when finished, especially on a shared computer.

Testing access

This is an 18+ testing preview linked to an existing Maroon Social account. University enrollment verification is not enabled yet. Do not treat a profile as proof that someone attends Texas A&M.
