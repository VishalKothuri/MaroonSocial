This file maps the original proposals in [ARCHITECTURE](ARCHITECTURE.md), [SCREENS-AND-FLOWS](SCREENS-AND-FLOWS.md), [TAG-AND-PRIVACY-UPDATE](TAG-AND-PRIVACY-UPDATE.md), and [SOCIAL-ADDITIONS](SOCIAL-ADDITIONS.md) to the development build. | Sync & caching | Incremental data API in two migrations, written but **not yet applied** (the MCP `apply_migration` call was declined twice): `20261005160000_feed_sync_incremental` and its review fixes `20261005170000_feed_sync_review_fixes`, to be applied together and in that order. `posts.changed_at` drives deltas but is never sent: each post carries `syncedAt`, the server time of the read, which orders two copies of a post without revealing when it changed. `changed_at` moves only for changes every reader can see: post edits/deletes, votes, replies, reply votes, poll votes, ready attachments (and the quote cards of that post), reposts, a rename (named posts, named replies and quotes of named posts only), a suspension (posts that visibly lose or regain a score, poll or quote, including posts the member voted on) and a block (only posts carrying a vote between the two members). Gateway actions `feed.page` (keyset `(created_at,id)`, 30 by default, 50 max, newest 50 replies per post plus `commentCount`), `feed.delta` (changed since a server clock with 5 s overlap, `removed` limited to ids the caller already holds, ≤300 known ids, `truncated` after 100, `resync`), `feed.posts`, `comments.page` and `room.messages` (`after_seq`/`before_seq`, ≤50, the room's metadata, and with `changed_since` the held messages deleted, edited or reacted to since then, via `messages.changed_at`). One projection (`post_json`/`comment_json`) backs every post read; `snapshot` carries only the first 30 posts plus `feedNext`/`serverNow`, and each conversation its newest 50 messages. The `social` function allowlists the five actions (deployed). Client: the snapshot runs on launch, foreground, after mutations and every 60 s (and on every tick while the feed still shows another community); the feed asks for a delta every 3 s while Community is visible; a truncated or resync delta re-reads the held pages (plus the first page after truncation) instead of collapsing the feed; an open chat polls `room.messages after_seq` with `changed_since` every 3 s, keeps the history it paged in across snapshots, and offers "Earlier messages" (`loadEarlierMessages`, `before_seq`); scrolling to the end loads the next page (`feedLoadMore`), while Search or Saved is on a "Search older posts" button (`feedLoadOlder`) does; Hot ranks the newest 150 posts and fills that window when chosen; a thread offers "Load earlier replies" (`loadEarlierReplies`) until it holds the first reply, pages back automatically for parents of the newest replies, and labels a parent that is merely not loaded "Reply to an earlier reply"; cards and thread headers count every reply (`commentCount`); a DM's "From this post" tag fetches the post by id. Retained tag/library pages refresh when their view asks or after a mutation, not on every poll. The cache file is written only when its bytes change (sets encode sorted, read times are not stored) and keeps the 100 newest feed posts (plus saved/own), 50 messages per room, and drops messages of rooms not opened for 30 days. | Until both migrations are applied the server answers the new actions with `invalid`; the app detects the missing `serverNow` and keeps the previous 3 s full-snapshot loop, so nothing regresses. Applying only the first migration would expose raw change times; apply the second right after it. Replies older than the newest 50 that are edited or deleted later are not re-synced until the thread is reloaded; a saved post's `saved` flag reaches the member's other devices with the snapshot or a page, not a delta. Hot no longer ranks beyond the newest 150 posts. Group display names and avatars in an open chat refresh with the snapshot. The rollback SQL suite and live runner are written but not yet run against the migrated schema. |
